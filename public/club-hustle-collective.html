About

We run & do fun things — all paces welcome. Hustle Collective is Chicago's crew that blurs the line between run club and good time. From lakefront 5Ks to Run to Rave events with DJ sets, we move as one and celebrate as one.

No pace requirements. No gatekeeping. Just Chicago energy, great people, and a party waiting at the finish line. Follow us on Instagram for event drops.
